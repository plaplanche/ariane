## Context

Ariane's design rests on two claims it does not yet measure: that a reviewer on a different model catches what the implementer missed, and that the failures that matter are failures of orchestration rather than of code. `ariane report` gives cost, duration, rounds and the first-pass verdict per ticket, but not how often a `go` was wrong, nor why tickets stopped. Without those numbers, choosing a reviewer model or a risk tier (#185) is guesswork. Spec: C19 (measurement, `ariane report`), C10 (review).

## Current state

- `src/ariane/report.py` reads `metrics.json` (outcome, rounds, first-pass verdict, sessions with model and cost) and the shadow records.
- A stopped ticket has an outcome (`stopped`, `needs a human`) and a reason in the journal, with no category.
- Nothing links a `go` to what happened after it (CI on the pull request, the owner's review, a later full replay).

## Decided spec

- **Review escapes.** `ariane report --escapes` (or a section of the normal report when the data exists) counts, per pair implementer model / reviewer model and per reviewer runtime: reviews, `go` verdicts, findings per review, and **escapes**: a `go` followed, on the same pull request, by a red CI on the reviewed head (read with #147's `ci_failures` when it exists, else skipped and said so), a "changes requested" review by a human, or the pull request closed without merge. The escape rate is escapes / `go`. The tracker reads these facts at report time; nothing is posted.
- **Stop categories.** Every outcome other than delivered green records a `category` in `metrics.json`, from a fixed list: `spec` (the issue gate or the implementer reported the ticket unclear), `guard` (push guard, tree changed), `budget` (cost or time limit), `runtime` (agent CLI error, login, quota), `checks` (blocking checks still red after the rounds), `review` (no-go after the rounds), `merge` (conflict in `update`), `ci` (red CI after the CI rounds), `infra` (setup failed, slot or lock unavailable). `ariane report` counts tickets per category and per model.
- **Comparison.** The report shows the rates side by side when two pairs (for example a different-model reviewer and a same-model reviewer configured on another run) have data, with the number of tickets behind each rate and no rate below 5 tickets.
- ADR (next free number): the escape definition, the categories, and why the report reads the tracker instead of storing outcomes Ariane did not see.
