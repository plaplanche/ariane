## Context

Selective checks (#184) and the risk tier (#185) save replay time by running fewer tests. That is only safe if what they miss is caught and measured: the owner needs to know how often a skipped check or a related-tests run let a failure through, before turning selection on for a project. This ticket adds a sampled full replay in the background of normal tickets, records the escapes, and reports the escape rate and the time saved. Spec: C9, C19 (measurement, `ariane report`); depends on #184 and #185.

## Current state

- After #184 and #185: a round may skip checks (`paths`) and run checks with `ARIANE_FULL_SUITE=0`; `checks.md` records both.
- `src/ariane/report.py` reports cost, duration, rounds and first-pass verdict per ticket; nothing measures what the replay did not run.
- The project's CI still runs the full suite on the pull request and on the base branch after merge: it is the safety net, but Ariane does not read its results.

## Decided spec

- **Shadow full replay.** When the last round of a ticket passed with at least one check skipped or run with `ARIANE_FULL_SUITE=0`, Ariane decides from `[checks_policy] full_replay_sample` (a fraction between 0 and 1, default 0.2; 0 disables it) whether to run a full replay: every check, `ARIANE_FULL_SUITE=1`, in fresh replay trees of the same commit, after the delivery (it never delays or changes the push, the pull request or the review). The sample is drawn deterministically from the ticket number and the commit, so a rerun gives the same choice.
- **Escapes.** A check that fails in the full replay after the selective round passed is an escape. Ariane records it in `work/<n>/full-replay.md` (which checks, skipped or partial, and their output, redacted), journals `ticket.checks.escape`, posts a comment on the pull request naming the failing check, and sets a failing commit status `ariane/full-replay`. A full replay that passes posts a success status.
- **Measures.** `measures.json` gains, per round: checks selected, skipped and partial, replay duration; and per ticket: whether a full replay ran, its duration and its escapes.
- **Report.** `ariane report` adds a "Selective checks" section: tickets with selection, replay time with selection versus the full replays' time on the sampled tickets, full replays run, escapes and escape rate (escapes / full replays), with a warning when the rate is above `[checks_policy] max_escape_rate` (default 0.02).
- ADR amendment to the selective checks ADR: why the full replay runs after delivery, why sampling and not every ticket, how the escape rate is meant to decide whether a project keeps selection on.
