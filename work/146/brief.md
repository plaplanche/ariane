## Decided spec

- **Conflict.** The merge is left in progress (no abort). One implementer session gets as its task, in an untrusted block (`kind="update"`): "Merge `<remote>/<base>` into this branch: resolve the conflicts below, keeping both sides' intent; do not commit", then the list of conflicting paths. After the session (guarded like every session), Ariane refuses to continue when unmerged paths remain (`git ls-files -u`) or a conflict marker line (`<<<<<<< `, `>>>>>>> `) is left in a conflicting path; otherwise it commits the merge with `git commit --no-edit` (the merge commit keeps both parents).
- **Red check after a clean merge.** One implementer session gets the failing blocking checks' names and outputs (the ends kept as in `checks.md`) in the same block, with "These checks fail after merging `<remote>/<base>`: fix them"; Ariane commits its work as `#<n>: fix after merging <base>`.
- **Then** the replay and the review run, and a `no-go` or red blocking checks go to fix rounds exactly as in `start` (`_check_and_deliver`), the update's implementer session counting as the first of the two rounds. Go and green: push as in #145. Otherwise: `needs a human`, nothing pushed.
- The prompt and the recorded context say this is an update, not a fresh ticket; the journal gets `ticket.update.implementer` with the task given.
- ADR 0033's amendment notes how the update session counts against the rounds and the conflict-marker check.
